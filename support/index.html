Help & support.

No sound?

Raise your iPhone's media volume and the Preview level in RotDeck Settings. Check whether audio is routed to Bluetooth, headphones, or AirPlay. Sounds play in silent mode because playback is intentional.

How do I share?

Touch and hold a pad, then choose Share audio. Saved combos also have a Share button. RotDeck exports a real WAV audio file through Apple's share sheet. Available destinations depend on the apps installed on your iPhone.

How do combos work?

Add two to six sounds, including repeats. Tap Reorder, drag the handles, then tap Done. Preview, name, and save your sequence. A short gap separates each clip. Editing and deleting are available from a saved combo's controls.

Why did playback stop?

Playback stops when RotDeck leaves the foreground, an interruption occurs, or headphones disconnect. It never resumes unexpectedly. Tap Play when you're ready.

What are the recordings?

The Studio originals pack contains original generated speech and a sustained cartoon shout synthesized from code. It includes no downloaded viral recordings or song excerpts. All ten sounds are credited in Settings and support audio sharing.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Sounds, favorites, combos, and exports are local. External source links and email use your chosen browser or mail app.
