Privacy policy.

Updated October 18, 2026 · RotDeck for iPhone

No collection or tracking by the app

RotDeck has no accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking, or runtime servers. The app does not collect or transmit personal information. Playback, favorites, combos, and audio export work offline.

Local storage

Favorites, saved combos, and preferences are stored in the app's private container on your iPhone. Device backups may include this container according to your Apple settings. The audio pack is bundled with the app.

Audio sharing

When you choose Share, RotDeck creates an audio file and opens Apple's share sheet. Your chosen destination receives the file and any included credit text. That destination's privacy policy applies. Temporary exports are removed after the sharing flow finishes.

Optional links and support

Source links open only when you tap them, using your selected browser. Email support opens your mail app. If you send an email, the information you voluntarily include is received by the support recipient and used to respond to your request. RotDeck never sends messages automatically.

Permissions

RotDeck does not request microphone, contacts, location, or photo-library access. It does not record your voice.

Your choices

You can reset your favorites, combos, and settings inside the app. Deleting the app removes its on-device container. For questions about information you included in support email, contact the address below.
